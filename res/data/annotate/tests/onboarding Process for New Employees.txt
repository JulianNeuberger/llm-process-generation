The onboarding process for new employees begins when a job offer is accepted. Initially, the HR department sends a welcome email containing essential information about the company, start date, and initial tasks. At the same time, the IT team is notified to set up necessary hardware and software accounts. On the first day, the new hire attends an orientation session conducted by HR, which covers company policies, work culture, and benefits. Meanwhile, the assigned department manager provides a personalized training schedule tailored to the employee's role. During the first week, new hires participate in team meetings and complete mandatory training modules online. The employee's progress is monitored through a feedback system, offering the opportunity for adjustments in the onboarding process based on initial adaptation and satisfaction. At the end of the first month, a review meeting with HR and the department manager is held to ensure the employee is settling in well and to address any concerns. This iterative process aims to integrate new employees effectively into the company culture and workflow.
