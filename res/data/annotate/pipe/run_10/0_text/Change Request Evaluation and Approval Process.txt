The Change Request Evaluation and Approval Process is a structured approach to managing proposed changes within an organization, ensuring that all modifications are thoroughly assessed and approved before implementation. This process begins with the submission of a Change Request Form, which must include a detailed description of the proposed change, the rationale behind it, and any potential impacts on current operations or systems. Once submitted, the request is reviewed by a Change Advisory Board (CAB), which consists of key stakeholders and subject matter experts. The CAB evaluates the request based on criteria such as feasibility, risk, cost, and alignment with organizational goals. If additional information is required, the request may be sent back to the originator for clarification. Upon satisfactory evaluation, the CAB makes a recommendation to either approve, reject, or defer the change. Approved changes are then prioritized and scheduled for implementation, with all relevant parties informed of the decision and any necessary preparations. Throughout the process, documentation is maintained to ensure transparency and accountability, and a post-implementation review is conducted to assess the effectiveness of the change and identify any lessons learned.
