The Network Incident Management Process is designed to efficiently handle and resolve network-related incidents to minimize downtime and ensure continuous service availability. The process begins with the detection of a network incident, which can be identified through automated monitoring systems or reported by users. Once an incident is detected, it is logged into the incident management system, and an initial assessment is conducted to determine its severity and impact. Meanwhile, a notification is sent to the network management team, who are responsible for prioritizing the incident based on its criticality. At the same time, a parallel workstream is initiated to gather relevant data and logs to assist in diagnosing the issue. Depending on the complexity of the incident, the team may decide to escalate it to higher-level support or specialized teams. While the resolution is being worked on, regular updates are communicated to stakeholders to keep them informed of the progress. Once the incident is resolved, the solution is documented, and a post-incident review is conducted to identify any lessons learned and preventive measures to avoid future occurrences. Finally, the incident is closed in the system, and any necessary follow-up actions are scheduled to ensure long-term network stability.
