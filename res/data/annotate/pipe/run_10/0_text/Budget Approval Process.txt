The budget approval process begins with the department heads preparing their budget proposals for the upcoming fiscal year. These proposals include detailed justifications for each line item, projected expenses, and anticipated revenue. Once the proposals are complete, they are submitted to the finance department for initial review. During this review, the finance team evaluates the proposals for accuracy, completeness, and alignment with the organization's financial goals. Meanwhile, the finance department prepares a consolidated budget report that includes all departmental proposals. At the same time, a parallel workstream involves the finance team conducting a financial analysis to assess the overall impact of the proposed budgets on the organization's financial health. After the initial review, the finance department schedules a meeting with the executive management team to discuss the consolidated budget report and the results of the financial analysis. During this meeting, the executive team may request revisions or additional information from the department heads. Once any necessary revisions are made, the final budget proposal is submitted to the board of directors for approval. The board reviews the proposal and may either approve it, request further modifications, or reject it entirely. If approved, the budget is implemented, and the finance department monitors expenditures throughout the year to ensure compliance with the approved budget.
