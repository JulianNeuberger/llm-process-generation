The lead generation and nurturing process begins with identifying potential customers through various channels such as social media, email campaigns, and online advertisements. Once potential leads are identified, they are entered into the customer relationship management (CRM) system for tracking and follow-up. At the same time, the marketing team creates personalized content tailored to the interests and needs of these leads, which is distributed through targeted email campaigns and social media posts. Meanwhile, the sales team evaluates the leads based on predefined criteria to determine their potential value and readiness to purchase. If a lead meets the criteria, it is assigned to a sales representative for direct engagement. If not, the lead continues to receive nurturing content until it is ready for sales engagement. Throughout this process, both teams collaborate to ensure that the messaging is consistent and that the leads are moving through the sales funnel efficiently. Regular meetings are held to review the progress of leads and adjust strategies as necessary to optimize conversion rates.
