The order fulfillment process in e-commerce begins when a customer places an order on the online platform. The system automatically verifies the payment details and confirms the order, triggering an inventory check to ensure the product is in stock. Once confirmed, the order details are sent to the warehouse where the item is picked, packed, and labeled for shipping. The logistics team then arranges for the shipment, selecting the most efficient courier service based on the delivery location and urgency. A tracking number is generated and sent to the customer via email or SMS, allowing them to monitor the delivery status. The package is dispatched and delivered to the customer's address, completing the transaction. Post-delivery, the system updates the inventory and records the transaction for future reference. Customer feedback is solicited to assess satisfaction and identify areas for improvement in the fulfillment process.
