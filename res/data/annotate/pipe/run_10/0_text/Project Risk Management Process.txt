The Project Risk Management Process is a systematic approach to identifying, analyzing, and responding to project risks to minimize their impact on project objectives. Initially, the project manager and the project team conduct a risk identification session to list potential risks that could affect the project. This is followed by a qualitative risk analysis, where risks are prioritized based on their probability and impact. Meanwhile, a quantitative risk analysis may be performed for high-priority risks to assess their potential effect on project timelines and costs. At the same time, risk response planning is undertaken to develop strategies for mitigating identified risks, which may include avoidance, transfer, mitigation, or acceptance. Once the risk responses are planned, the project manager integrates these strategies into the project plan and communicates them to all stakeholders. During project execution, risk monitoring and control activities are carried out to track identified risks, monitor residual risks, and identify new risks, ensuring that risk response plans are effectively implemented. If a risk materializes, the project team executes the predefined response plan and evaluates its effectiveness, making adjustments as necessary. Throughout the project lifecycle, risk management activities are documented and reviewed to ensure continuous improvement in managing project risks.
