The order fulfillment process in a logistics company begins with the receipt of a customer order, which is entered into the company's order management system. Once the order is confirmed, the inventory is checked to ensure the availability of the requested items. If the items are in stock, the warehouse team is notified to pick and pack the products. Meanwhile, the shipping department prepares the necessary documentation for the shipment, including labels and customs forms if required. At the same time, the finance department generates an invoice and sends it to the customer. If the items are not in stock, the procurement team is alerted to reorder the necessary products from suppliers, which may introduce a delay in the process. Once the products are packed, they are handed over to the shipping team, who coordinates with logistics partners to ensure timely delivery to the customer. Throughout the process, the customer is kept informed of the order status via automated notifications. Upon successful delivery, the customer is asked for feedback to assess satisfaction and identify areas for improvement in the logistics process.
