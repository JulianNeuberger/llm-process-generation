The waste management and recycling process begins with the collection of waste materials from designated collection points within the facility. These materials are then transported to the sorting area, where they are categorized into recyclable and non-recyclable items. While the sorting team is working on categorizing the waste, the logistics team simultaneously coordinates with recycling partners to schedule the pickup of sorted recyclable materials. At the same time, non-recyclable waste is prepared for disposal in compliance with environmental regulations. Once the sorting is complete, recyclable materials are temporarily stored in a designated area until they are picked up by the recycling partners. Meanwhile, the environmental compliance officer reviews the waste management records to ensure all procedures adhere to the regulatory standards. If any discrepancies are found, corrective actions are initiated to address the issues. The process concludes with a monthly review meeting where the waste management team evaluates the efficiency of the process and discusses potential improvements to enhance recycling rates and reduce overall waste.
