The employee onboarding and training process is designed to integrate new hires into the company culture and equip them with the necessary skills and knowledge to perform their roles effectively. This process begins with a welcome orientation session, where new employees are introduced to the company's mission, values, and organizational structure. Following the orientation, each new hire is assigned a mentor from their department who will guide them through their initial weeks. The training program includes a combination of e-learning modules, hands-on training sessions, and shadowing experienced colleagues. Progress is monitored through regular check-ins with the mentor and HR, ensuring that any questions or concerns are addressed promptly. The process concludes with a feedback session where the new employee can provide insights on their onboarding experience, allowing the company to continuously improve the process.
