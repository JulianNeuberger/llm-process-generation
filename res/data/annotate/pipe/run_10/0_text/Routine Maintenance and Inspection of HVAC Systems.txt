The routine maintenance and inspection of HVAC systems in a facility is a critical process to ensure optimal performance and energy efficiency. The process begins with scheduling regular maintenance checks, which are typically conducted quarterly. A certified HVAC technician is assigned to inspect the system, starting with a thorough examination of the air filters, which are cleaned or replaced as necessary. Meanwhile, the technician checks the thermostat settings to ensure they are functioning correctly and recalibrates them if needed. At the same time, the technician inspects the electrical connections, tightening any loose connections and measuring voltage and current on motors to prevent future failures. During the inspection, the technician also examines the system's ductwork for any signs of leaks or blockages, which are addressed immediately to maintain airflow efficiency. If any major issues are identified, a decision is made to either repair or replace the faulty components, depending on the severity and cost-effectiveness of the solution. After completing the inspection and any necessary repairs, the technician documents the findings and actions taken in a maintenance log, which is reviewed by the facility manager to ensure compliance with safety and operational standards. This comprehensive process not only extends the lifespan of the HVAC system but also contributes to a comfortable and safe environment for all facility occupants.
