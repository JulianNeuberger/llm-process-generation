The product concept development and validation process begins with the identification of market needs and customer requirements through comprehensive market research and customer feedback analysis. Once the needs are clearly defined, a cross-functional team, including members from marketing, design, engineering, and finance, collaborates to brainstorm and generate innovative product ideas. These ideas are then evaluated based on feasibility, potential market impact, and alignment with the company's strategic goals. The most promising concept is selected for further development, and a detailed product specification document is created, outlining the key features, design elements, and performance criteria. Prototypes are developed and subjected to rigorous testing to ensure they meet the specified requirements and perform reliably under various conditions. Feedback from these tests is used to refine the product design. Concurrently, a business case is developed to assess the financial viability of the product, including cost estimates, pricing strategy, and projected return on investment. The final step involves presenting the validated product concept and business case to senior management for approval to proceed to the next phase of product development.
