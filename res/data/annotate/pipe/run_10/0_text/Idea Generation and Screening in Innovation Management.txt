The process of idea generation and screening in innovation management begins with the identification of a strategic area or problem that requires innovative solutions. This is followed by a brainstorming session involving cross-functional teams to generate a wide range of ideas. Each idea is documented with a brief description and potential impact. The next step involves a preliminary screening where ideas are evaluated based on criteria such as feasibility, alignment with company goals, and potential market impact. A scoring system is often used to rank the ideas, and those that meet the threshold are selected for further development. The selected ideas are then subjected to a more detailed analysis, including market research and technical feasibility studies. Feedback from stakeholders is gathered to refine the ideas further. The process concludes with a presentation to the innovation committee, where the most promising ideas are approved for prototyping and testing.
