The New Product Development Process in the Research and Development department begins with the identification of market needs and opportunities through comprehensive market research and analysis. Once a potential product idea is identified, a cross-functional team is assembled, including members from R&D, marketing, and finance, to conduct a feasibility study and develop a business case. This is followed by the concept development phase, where the team brainstorms and refines product concepts, creating prototypes and conducting initial testing to evaluate functionality and design. After successful concept validation, the process moves into the detailed design and development phase, where the product specifications are finalized, and a working prototype is developed. This prototype undergoes rigorous testing and evaluation to ensure it meets all regulatory and quality standards. Concurrently, the marketing team develops a go-to-market strategy, including pricing, distribution, and promotional plans. Once the product passes all tests and receives necessary approvals, it enters the production phase, where manufacturing processes are established, and the product is prepared for launch. The final step involves a post-launch review to assess the product's performance in the market and gather feedback for future improvements. Throughout the process, regular project reviews and updates are conducted to ensure alignment with strategic goals and to address any challenges that arise.
