The procurement process for supplier selection and contracting begins with the identification of the need for goods or services within the organization. This need is documented and approved by the relevant department heads. Once approved, a request for proposal (RFP) is prepared, detailing the specifications, requirements, and criteria for supplier selection. The RFP is then distributed to potential suppliers, who are invited to submit their proposals. Meanwhile, a cross-functional team is assembled to evaluate the proposals based on predefined criteria such as cost, quality, delivery time, and supplier reputation. At the same time, the procurement team conducts a preliminary assessment of the suppliers' financial stability and compliance with regulatory requirements. After the evaluation, the team shortlists the most suitable suppliers and conducts negotiations to finalize terms and conditions. A decision is made to either proceed with a single supplier or to engage multiple suppliers based on the organization's strategic objectives. Once a supplier is selected, a contract is drafted, reviewed by the legal department, and signed by both parties. The procurement process concludes with the establishment of a supplier relationship management plan to ensure ongoing performance monitoring and continuous improvement.
