The product development lifecycle management process is a comprehensive approach to overseeing the entire journey of a product from conception to market release. This process begins with the ideation phase, where potential product ideas are generated and evaluated based on market research and customer feedback. Once a viable idea is selected, the design and prototyping phase commences, involving detailed design work and the creation of prototypes for testing. During this phase, cross-functional teams collaborate to ensure the product meets technical specifications and customer needs. Following successful prototyping, the development phase involves refining the product design and preparing for mass production. This includes sourcing materials, setting up manufacturing processes, and conducting quality assurance tests to ensure the product meets all regulatory and safety standards. The final phase is the launch and post-launch evaluation, where the product is introduced to the market, and its performance is monitored. Feedback from customers and sales data are analyzed to make any necessary adjustments or improvements. Throughout the entire lifecycle, project management tools and methodologies are employed to track progress, manage resources, and ensure timely delivery of the product to market.
