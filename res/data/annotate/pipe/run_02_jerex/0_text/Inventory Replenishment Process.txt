The inventory replenishment process begins with the monitoring of stock levels through an automated inventory management system, which triggers a reorder point when stock falls below a predetermined threshold. At this point, a purchase order is automatically generated and sent to the supplier, detailing the required quantities and specifications. Meanwhile, the procurement team reviews the purchase order for accuracy and compliance with budgetary constraints. Once approved, the supplier receives the order and confirms the delivery schedule. At the same time, the logistics team prepares for the incoming shipment by allocating warehouse space and scheduling staff for unloading and inspection. Upon arrival, the goods are inspected for quality and quantity, and any discrepancies are reported back to the supplier for resolution. The inventory management system is then updated to reflect the new stock levels, and the replenishment cycle is completed. This process ensures that inventory levels are maintained efficiently, minimizing stockouts and overstock situations.
