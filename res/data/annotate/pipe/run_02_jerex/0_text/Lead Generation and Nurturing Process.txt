The lead generation and nurturing process begins with identifying potential customers through various channels such as social media, email campaigns, and online advertisements. Once potential leads are identified, they are entered into the customer relationship management (CRM) system for tracking and further engagement. At the same time, the marketing team creates personalized content tailored to the interests and needs of these leads, which is distributed through targeted email campaigns and social media posts. Meanwhile, the sales team evaluates the leads based on predefined criteria to determine their potential value and readiness to purchase. If a lead meets the criteria, it is passed on to the sales team for direct engagement and conversion efforts. If not, the lead continues to receive nurturing content until it is ready for sales engagement. Throughout this process, data analytics tools are used to monitor the effectiveness of the campaigns and adjust strategies as needed to optimize lead conversion rates.
