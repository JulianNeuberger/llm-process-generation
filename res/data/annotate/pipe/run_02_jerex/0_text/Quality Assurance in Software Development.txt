The quality assurance process in software development is a systematic approach to ensure that the software product meets the specified requirements and is free of defects. This process begins with the planning phase, where quality objectives and criteria are defined based on the project requirements. During the design phase, quality assurance teams work closely with developers to ensure that the design specifications align with the quality standards. The implementation phase involves rigorous testing, including unit testing, integration testing, and system testing, to identify and rectify any defects. Quality assurance specialists use automated testing tools and manual testing techniques to verify the functionality, performance, and security of the software. Throughout the process, documentation is maintained to track defects, testing results, and corrective actions. Regular audits and reviews are conducted to ensure compliance with quality standards and to identify areas for improvement. The process concludes with a final quality assessment and the release of the software, accompanied by a detailed report on the quality assurance activities and outcomes.
