The regulatory compliance audit process begins with the planning phase, where the compliance team identifies the scope of the audit, including the specific regulations and standards that need to be reviewed. During this phase, the team also schedules the audit timeline and allocates resources. Once planning is complete, the audit execution phase commences, where auditors collect and review documentation, conduct interviews with relevant personnel, and perform site inspections to ensure adherence to regulatory requirements. Meanwhile, the compliance team prepares a preliminary report highlighting any potential non-compliance issues. At the same time, a parallel workstream involves the legal department reviewing any recent changes in regulations that might impact the audit findings. After the execution phase, the audit findings are compiled into a comprehensive report, which is then reviewed by senior management. A decision point occurs here, where management can either approve the report as is or request further investigation into specific areas of concern. If further investigation is required, the compliance team revisits the audit findings and provides additional clarification or evidence. Once the report is approved, the final phase involves implementing corrective actions for any identified non-compliance issues, with the compliance team monitoring the progress of these actions to ensure they are completed within the stipulated timeframe. The process concludes with a follow-up audit to verify the effectiveness of the corrective actions and to ensure ongoing compliance with regulatory standards.
