The employee onboarding process begins with the Human Resources department sending a welcome email to the new hire, which includes essential information such as the start date, time, and location, as well as any necessary documentation that needs to be completed before the first day. Meanwhile, the IT department is notified to set up the new employee's workstation, including computer, phone, and access to necessary software and systems. On the first day, the new hire attends an orientation session conducted by HR, where they are introduced to company policies, culture, and benefits. At the same time, the new employee's manager prepares a schedule for the first week, which includes meetings with key team members and training sessions. After the orientation, the new hire meets with their manager to discuss job responsibilities and expectations. Throughout the first month, HR conducts regular check-ins to address any questions or concerns the new employee may have, ensuring a smooth transition into the company.
