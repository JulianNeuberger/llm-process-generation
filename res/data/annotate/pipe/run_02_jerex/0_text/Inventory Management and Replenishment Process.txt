The inventory management and replenishment process begins with the monitoring of stock levels through an integrated inventory management system, which provides real-time data on current stock quantities and alerts for low inventory levels. When stock levels reach a predefined threshold, a purchase order is automatically generated and sent to the supplier. Meanwhile, the procurement team reviews the purchase order for accuracy and ensures that it aligns with budgetary constraints and supplier agreements. At the same time, the warehouse team prepares for the incoming shipment by organizing storage space and updating the inventory system to reflect the expected delivery. Upon receipt of the goods, the warehouse team conducts a quality check to ensure that the items meet the required standards and specifications. If the goods pass the quality check, they are added to the inventory, and the system is updated to reflect the new stock levels. In the event of any discrepancies or quality issues, the procurement team is notified to resolve the matter with the supplier. This process ensures that inventory levels are maintained efficiently, minimizing stockouts and overstock situations while optimizing storage space and resources.
